A recent report reveals that 90% of banking scam attempts now occur on mobile devices, highlighting the growing role of smartphones in financial fraud. Researchers found that scammers are increasingly exploiting mobile banking interactions through social engineering, impersonation, and deceptive communications to manipulate victims into authorizing fraudulent transactions. These attacks often rely on convincing users to act voluntarily, making them difficult to detect through traditional security measures.
