In an article published by HackRead, the limitations of Mobile Device Management (MDM) are examined as organizations face increasingly sophisticated threats targeting employee smartphones and corporate data. While MDM helps enforce device policies and manage access, it does not eliminate risks from mobile phishing, malicious applications, and compromised devices. The article highlights the importance of a broader mobile security strategy that combines device management with continuous threat detection and response.
